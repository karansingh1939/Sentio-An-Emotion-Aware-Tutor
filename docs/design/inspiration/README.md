## Ideas worth taking so far

- **Footnote every claim**, as Apple does, with Sentio's real measured results and their caveats.
- **Consistent interaction feel:** hover and press states on everything you can point at, one shared speed and easing, and menus that wait a moment before opening.
- **At most one dark, cinematic moment** inside an otherwise light, calm page, rather than a fully dark page.
- **One short line per headline**, with real Sentio screenshots as the imagery.

## Rules for new references

- Name the source and the date.
- Measure the real styles rather than guessing from a screenshot.
- Separate what is good from what conflicts with Sentio's product truth (no invented proof, local install instead of sign-up).
