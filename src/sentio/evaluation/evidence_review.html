Can these notes answer the question?

This is label review for Sentio’s evidence experiment. You are judging the supplied passage, not writing an answer or checking an AI prediction.

Three simple steps

  1. Read the question and the whole passage.
  2. Decide whether the passage contains every fact needed to answer.
  3. Point to the evidence, explain the missing fact, or skip if unsure.
Examples before you start

Enough evidence: Question: “What is the mean of 2 and 4?” Passage: “The mean is the sum divided by the number of values.” Rationale: “The formula lets me compute (2 + 4) / 2 = 3.” Simple arithmetic is allowed.

Missing evidence: Question: “What was our model’s measured accuracy?” Passage: “Accuracy is the fraction of predictions that are correct.” Rationale: “The definition is present, but our experiment’s result is missing.”

Unclear question: “How does it affect that?” Without clear referents, the passage cannot support a definite answer. Choose missing evidence and explain what needs clarification.

Use only this passage. Familiarity with the topic is not evidence that the passage answers it. Do not guess. Small batches are fine; these labels are not independent expert ground truth.
